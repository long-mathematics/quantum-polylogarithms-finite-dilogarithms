# Initial public research drafts — v0.1.0

Two manuscripts by Christopher D. Long, released October 1, 2026:

1. **Rational-Exponential Iterated Integrals and Quantum Polylogarithms** — absolute fixed-parameter functional completeness and a separate pentagon crossed-product normal form.
2. **Finite Quantum Dilogarithms** — raw-equation normalization exceptions, explicit low-order scheme certificates, effective degree bounds, and a conditional infinitesimal rigidity criterion.

Both LaTeX sources, compiled PDFs, verification scripts, and actual check outputs are included. The fixed-parameter manuscript is based on the later sole-author version 2, not the earlier relative-only draft.

**Mathematical status:** the general finite-étale result in the second paper is conditional on a specified realization/reconstruction hypothesis over dual numbers. The low-order reducedness certificates and effective degree bounds are independent of that hypothesis. Neither paper asserts arithmetic specialization, Stark reciprocity, or the required unitary Galois-conjugate theorem.

References and provenance have been updated against Radchenko–Wheeler v2 and Appleby–Flammia–Kopp v1. The finite pentagon, algebraicity/finiteness, all-rank twisted convolution, and the cocycle dictionary are cited background. No literature-wide priority claim is made.

Exact symbolic checks, low-order saturated Gröbner certificates, selected numerical contour comparisons, and both PDF builds passed in the release preparation. Numerical checks are not proofs or interval enclosures. These are AI-assisted, internally checked research drafts, not independently refereed or formally verified papers.
