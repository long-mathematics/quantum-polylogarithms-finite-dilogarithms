# Focused audit: realization and normalized reconstruction

Date: October 1, 2026. Scope: Steps 2 and 5 of the proposed proof discharging the initial finite-paper Hypothesis 3.1. This is an internal mathematical audit, not independent peer review or formal verification.

## Sources and baseline

The baseline is the initial repository release, commit `9dd89632ae0ca50b65fd84c93cffb8d1be34e1eb`. The primary sources are [RW v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21892v2), Sections 5.4–5.5 and Appendix B, and [ENO, On fusion categories](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/wp-content/uploads/annals-v162-n2-p01.pdf), Theorem 2.27 and Section 7.1. The original field-extension remark does not itself cover nonreduced rings.

## Step 2: nonreduced-base realization

**Finding.** The displayed completeness expansion in RW Appendix B.1, printed PDF page 30, contains `N(N+d)/d^2` multiplying the sum of `S_h S_h'`. The coefficient obtained directly from the generator formulas is `N/d^2 + N/d = N(1+d)/d^2 = 1`. The printed expression has residual `N(N-1)/d^2` under `d^2=N(d+1)`. This corrects an intermediate calculation, not the generator formulas or the construction.

**Repair incorporated.** The manuscript works in a fixed-sector normalized coordinate ring before taking any radical. It proves the correlation identity by finite Fourier inversion, derives the dual pentagon by an invertible Fourier transformation (not conjugation), lists all generator formulas, gives the corrected completeness expansion, and identifies the Fourier/correlation/Gaussian/dual-pentagon reductions in the fusion proof. Final cancellations use completeness, not cancellation of a possible zero divisor. Split maps and snake identities are retained explicitly.

**Flatness.** The dual-number Hom-space proof uses freeness of the ambient Leavitt algebra, scalar endomorphisms and zero cross-Hom spaces on the special fiber, and explicit split fusion. No assertion of semisimplicity over the dual numbers or arbitrary-base preservation of kernels is used.

## Step 5: invariance under monoidal gauge

**Finding.** Independent rescalings `T_g -> u_g T_g` change the reconstructed scalar by `u_g/u_0`. Merely counting primed and unprimed factors therefore does not establish invariance. Also, a nonzero element of a rank-one module over the dual numbers need not generate it.

**Repair incorporated.** The character-average projector identifies the distinguished line as the free summand `A T_0`. Its selected element must be a generator (nonzero reduction). The isomorphisms defining `U_g` are normalized by their action on `Hom(1,rho^2)`, forcing a single common rescaling of the entire `T_g` family. The reconstructed scalar includes the inverse associator. Transport under an A-linear strong monoidal equivalence includes the tensorator and unit constraint, preserves the distinguished line and normalized `U_g`, and yields equality of scalars in A, not just modulo epsilon.

The direct Leavitt contraction leaves exactly `E_A(g)/sqrt(N)`. The ENO trivialization is taken on the fixed labelled skeleton and reduces to the identity; no discrete automorphism of the group moves the labels.

## Consequence and limits

With the repairs above, the proof discharges the original realization hypothesis. ENO first-order rigidity kills all nonexceptional tangent vectors; exact small-order Jacobian certificates handle the exceptional points. Nakayama's lemma and descent give finite étaleness of the actual raw scheme. No arithmetic-specialization functor, Stark reciprocity, or unitary Galois-conjugate theorem follows from this argument.
